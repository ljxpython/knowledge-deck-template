01 / Frame

Start with the decision, not the tool.

Tool comparisons get noisy when the actual constraint stays implicit. Give the decision a name, its owner, and the consequence of being wrong.

Question

What changes?

State the action the team must choose.

Constraint

What is scarce?

Make time, reversibility, or operating cost concrete.

Trigger

What revisits it?

Record the observation that invalidates the choice.
